## Adding a picture or a GIF

Click into the page where you want it, then paste it (Ctrl+V) or drag the file in from a folder.
It is filed away next to this page automatically and a line like this appears:

`![What the picture shows](images/example.png)`

The words in the square brackets are read aloud by screen readers and shown if the picture will not load, so describe what it shows rather than writing "image".
