Hand tools are manually operated tools used for cutting, drilling, fastening, shaping, measuring, and assembling materials. They are commonly used in workshops for fabrication, maintenance, and basic mechanical work.

**Examples**: Allen key, file, screwdriver, hacksaw, hand drill, pliers, spanner, etc.

## Allen Key

An Allen key is an L-shaped hexagonal tool used to tighten or loosen bolts and screws with a hexagonal socket.

*Example: Used for tightening socket-head screws in robots and machinery.*

![An Allen key, used to tighten and loosen hexagonal socket-head screws.](/img/mechanical/phase4-hand-tool-allen-key.jpg)

## Files

A file is a hand tool with a rough, toothed surface used to remove small amounts of material and smooth or shape edges.

*Example: Used for deburring and finishing metal or plastic parts.*

![A set of files, used to smooth and shape material edges.](/img/mechanical/phase4-hand-tool-files.jpg)

## Screwdriver

A screwdriver is a hand tool used to tighten or loosen screws by applying rotational force.

*Example: Flat-head and Phillips screwdrivers are commonly used in mechanical and electrical work.*

![A screwdriver, used to tighten and loosen screws.](/img/mechanical/phase4-hand-tool-screwdriver.jpg)

## Hand Drill

A hand drill is a portable tool used to make holes in materials using a rotating drill bit.

*Example: Used for drilling holes in wood, plastic, and metal sheets.*

![A hand drill, used to make holes with a rotating bit.](/img/mechanical/phase4-hand-tool-hand-drill.jpg)

## Tapping Machine

A tapping machine is a tool used to cut internal threads inside a pre-drilled hole using a tap.

*Example: Used to create threads for bolts and screws in metal components.*

![A tapping machine, used to cut internal threads.](/img/mechanical/phase4-hand-tool-tapping-machine.jpg)

## Hacksaw

A hacksaw is a manual cutting tool with a fine-toothed metal blade used to cut materials such as metal, plastic, and pipes.

*Example: Used to cut metal rods, bolts, and PVC pipes.*

![A hacksaw, used to cut metal, plastic, and pipes.](/img/mechanical/phase4-hand-tool-hacksaw.jpg)
