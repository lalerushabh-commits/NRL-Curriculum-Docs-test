Champions are not lucky. They check.

Everything this book teaches compresses into one printed page: the inspection you run before every match. Two people, three minutes, out loud — one reads, one checks and answers. Laminate it. Live by it.

| # | Check | Pass looks like |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Battery charged | Pack reads ~12.4 V+ on the **battery checker**; Controller charged too |
| 2 | Battery secured | Strap tight; XT30 clicked; cable strain-relieved |
| 3 | Full connector patrol | Every connector: look · seat · tug ([Module 5.1](/phase-5-wiring-wireless-and-the-whole-robot/connectors-and-the-wiring-harness)) |
| 4 | Harness survey | No new pinches, rubs, or loose ties since last match ([Module 5.2](/phase-5-wiring-wireless-and-the-whole-robot/wiring-the-robot)) |
| 5 | Antenna | High, clear, on the polycarbonate plate, cable secured ([Module 5.3](/phase-5-wiring-wireless-and-the-whole-robot/hexa-link-and-the-antenna)) |
| 6 | Power-up & pairing | Screens alive; OLED paired; TFT link healthy ([Module 5.4](/phase-5-wiring-wireless-and-the-whole-robot/lights-screens-and-telemetry)) |
| 7 | Idle telemetry | Voltage healthy and steady; idle current small ([Module 3.4](/phase-3-power/brownouts-and-power-monitoring)) |
| 8 | Motion test | Brief joystick check: correct wheels, correct directions; servos sweep freely |
| 9 | Sensor sanity | ARC-8 over the mat shows sensible values; IMU steady ([Module 4.7](/phase-4-motion-and-sensing/the-imu), [Module 4.8](/phase-4-motion-and-sensing/the-arc-8-line-sensor)) |
| 10 | Fault log reviewed | Yesterday's gremlins confirmed fixed, not merely forgotten |

:::tip[Own the clipboard]
Make one electronics student the **inspector of record** each match day — rotating the role builds the whole team. The inspector signs the sheet. It feels official because it is: this is exactly how real engineering teams operate.
:::
