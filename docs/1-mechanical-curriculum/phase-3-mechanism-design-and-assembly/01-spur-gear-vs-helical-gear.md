## Introduction

Gears transmit power and rotational motion between shafts. The two most common cylindrical gears are spur gears and helical gears.

## Spur Gear

- Straight teeth parallel to the shaft.
- Entire tooth engages at once.
- High efficiency (97–99%).
- Simple, inexpensive and easy to manufacture.
- Produces more noise and vibration at high speed.

![A spur gear, with straight teeth parallel to the shaft.](/img/mechanical/phase3-spur-gear.png)

## Helical Gear

- Teeth are cut at an angle (helix angle).
- Teeth engage gradually, making operation smoother and quieter.
- Higher load carrying capacity.
- Longer service life due to better load distribution.
- Generates axial thrust that must be supported by bearings.

![A helical gear, with teeth cut at an angle.](/img/mechanical/phase3-helical-gear.png)

## Axial Thrust Force

The angled teeth create a force along the shaft in addition to the tangential force. This axial thrust is managed using thrust bearings, angular contact bearings, tapered roller bearings, or double-helical gears.

## Spur vs Helical Gear

| Feature | Spur | Helical |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Teeth | Straight | Angled |
| Engagement | Instant | Gradual |
| Noise | High | Low |
| Vibration | High | Low |
| Load Capacity | Moderate | High |
| Axial Thrust | None | Present |
| Cost | Lower | Higher |
| Applications | Low/Medium speed | Medium/High speed |

## Important Gear Parameters

### 1. Module (m)

Defines tooth size.
**Formula**: m = d / z
Larger module means larger, stronger but heavier teeth.

### 2. Number of Teeth (z)

Determines gear ratio, output speed and torque.

### 3. Pitch Diameter (d)

Effective rolling diameter of the gear.
**Formula**: d = m × z

### 4. Helix Angle (ψ)

Typically 15°–30° for competition robots. Higher helix angles provide smoother engagement but increase axial thrust.

### 5. Face Width

Width of the gear tooth. A common guideline is 8–12 × module for good strength and load distribution.

![The key gear parameters: module, teeth, pitch diameter, helix angle, and face width.](/img/mechanical/phase3-gear-parameters.png)

## Summary

Spur gears are simple, efficient and economical, while helical gears offer smoother, quieter operation and higher load capacity. The trade-off is axial thrust. Key parameters every student should understand are module, number of teeth, pitch diameter, helix angle and face width.
